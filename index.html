Cities

A city is a large, permanent human settlement with a high population density and complex infrastructure.
It is much bigger and more important than a town or a village.

Core features of a city include:

  • High density in population with people living and working close together.
  • Advanced infrastructure like public transport, clean water and electricity

The legal definition for what makes a place a "city" changes throughout the world.
For example, a town in the United Kingdom only becomes a city through a special royal title granted by the Crown. While in the United States a city is an incorporated area with its own local government and charter granted by the state.
